# Act One Spirit Rank 2 Design

After defeating the Orc Brothers, each player completes this fixed sequence:

1. Choose one normal reward. Exactly one of the three offers is a randomly selected Rank 2 upgrade for an owned Rank 1 ability when an eligible ability exists.
2. Their equipped Spirit advances to Rank 2. This is not a choice; a single confirmation card clearly shows the passive and active improvements.
3. Both non-permanently-dead heroes return to living form and visibly restore to full HP and PP.
4. Act 2 begins at The Mountain Pass.

Spirit Rank 2 never adds active uses or changes action timing. A spent Phoenix Rebirth remains spent.

## Rank 2 values

- Bloodpact: round Barrier 2 → 4; active Barrier 30 → 45. HP costs remain 1 and 10.
- Embertail: opener becomes 10 + 30% Fire Power and applies Burn; active becomes 10 + 45% Fire Power to all enemies and applies Burn.
- Stoneback: first Barrier bonus 5 → 10; active becomes 12 + 35% Earth Power Barrier to both heroes.
- Swift Wing: first-action chance 20% → 35%; active becomes 28 + 80% Wind Power damage.
- Riverheart: party healing bonus 2 → 4; active becomes 28 + 65% Water Power healing.
- Warhound: its Quick Auto Attack deals 60% more damage; active becomes 28 + 110% Attack damage.
- Guard Dog: passive damage 5 → 8; the first attack stopped by Bodyguard is countered for 12 fixed Spirit Physical damage.
- Ironquill: each Steel Dart grants 15% instead of 10%; the active's three darts deal 8 damage each.
- Phoenix: fixed round healing 2 → 4, Phoenix Tonic bonus 10 → 20, and an unspent Rebirth returns with 55 HP and 20 Barrier.
